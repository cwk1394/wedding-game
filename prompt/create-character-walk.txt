[FRAME SPACING / SAFE MARGIN]
────────────────────

THIS IS EXTREMELY IMPORTANT FOR SPRITE SLICING.

Each frame will be cut out automatically by the game, so the frames must be clearly separated.

- Divide the canvas into exactly 4 equal-width frame cells.
- Place ONE character in the horizontal center of each cell.
- Keep a clear, EMPTY pure-white gap between neighboring characters.
  The gap must be at least 15% of a single cell's width.
- Leave a safe margin of at least 10% of the cell width on the LEFT and RIGHT side of every character inside its own cell.
- NO part of a character may cross into, touch, or overlap a neighboring cell:
  this includes long hair, flowing hair strands, ponytails, dresses, skirts, capes, ribbons, sleeves, arms, legs, weapons, and accessories.
- Swinging hair or clothing must stay entirely inside the character's own cell.
- If the character has long hair or a long dress, scale the character slightly smaller so the margins are preserved rather than letting it touch the next frame.
- Do NOT draw separators, borders, or grid lines between cells. The gap must be plain white background only.
