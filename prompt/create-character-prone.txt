[PRONE POSE SPEC]
────────────────────

* Direction: The character's HEAD is on the LEFT side and the feet are on the RIGHT side. The character faces LEFT.
* Pose: Lying flat on the stomach, body stretched horizontally, close to the ground.
  - chest and belly touching the ground line
  - head slightly raised, face visible in left-facing profile / three-quarter view
  - arms bent forward under or in front of the head, elbows on the ground
  - legs stretched straight back to the right

* Clothing: Dresses, skirts, and coats should lie naturally along the body and stay inside the frame cell.

[2-FRAME CYCLE]
────────────────────

The two frames form a subtle, calm idle loop while lying down:

FRAME 1 — Resting prone pose.

FRAME 2 — Same pose with a very small breathing motion:
- chest/shoulders raised by 1–2 pixels
- head raised by 1–2 pixels

Everything else stays identical. The loop must be seamless: Frame 1 → Frame 2 → Frame 1.

────────────────────
[CRITICAL FRAME ALIGNMENT]
────────────────────

The character must stay at EXACTLY THE SAME X AND Y POSITION in both frames.

- same ground line (the bottom of the body touches the same baseline in both frames)
- same horizontal position
- same scale
- same bounding-box center

Only the tiny breathing motion may change between the frames.

────────────────────
[FRAME SPACING / SAFE MARGIN]
────────────────────

THIS IS EXTREMELY IMPORTANT FOR SPRITE SLICING.

Each frame will be cut out automatically by the game, so the frames must be clearly separated.

- Divide the canvas into exactly 2 equal-width frame cells.
- Place ONE character in the horizontal center of each cell.
- The spacing must be IDENTICAL: both characters sit at the same position inside their own cell, so the distance between the two characters equals the distance between cell centers.
- Keep a clear, EMPTY pure-white gap between the two characters.
  The gap must be at least 15% of a single cell's width.
- Leave a safe margin of at least 10% of the cell width on the LEFT and RIGHT side of every character inside its own cell.
- NO part of a character may cross into, touch, or overlap the neighboring cell:
  this includes long hair, flowing hair strands, dresses, skirts, capes, ribbons, sleeves, arms, legs, weapons, and accessories.
- If the character has long hair or a long dress, scale the character slightly smaller so the margins are preserved rather than letting it touch the other frame.
- Do NOT draw separators, borders, or grid lines between cells. The gap must be plain white background only.
